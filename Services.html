Voice of Wits is the official campus radio station, dedicated to keeping students informed, connected, and entertained. The station offers daily updates on everything happening around campus, from events and announcements to student life highlights, so no one misses a beat. Beyond broadcasting, Voice of Wits opens doors for students through internship and learning opportunities, giving aspiring broadcasters, journalists, and media enthusiasts hands-on experience in a real radio environment. And of course, the station keeps the campus vibe alive with a great mix of good music, making it the go-to soundtrack for student life at Wits.
